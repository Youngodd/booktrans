§ 02

API key

Book text is sent to the provider you pick below, and nowhere else; the app has no server and no telemetry. You are responsible for having the right to translate the books you use.

Domestic and international Qwen keys are different; pick the site your key came from.

How do I get a key?

Checked with the provider, then stored in your system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager); never written to a file or logged.

About

Book Translator, free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0. Source code: github.com/Youngodd/booktrans. It bundles PyMuPDF (AGPL, Artifex).

Good to know

Cost is billed to your own key. Translation quality can vary; check important pages. Closing the app pauses a running book; open it again and press Continue.
